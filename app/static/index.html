How it works, in plain words

  1. Market: load the price history of an index or stock.
  2. Find Patterns: for every trading day, the app calculates where all the grahas were (by modern astronomy or the Surya Siddhanta) and turns that into about 1,300 yes/no facts: Jupiter in Taurus, Mars retrograde, Yoga Siddhi, Saturn in the 12th from the Moon, and so on. It then tests every fact, and every pair of facts, against whether the market rose or fell next.
  3. Pattern Library: whatever survives is saved as a pattern set.
  4. Predict: the app calculates future planet positions and applies the saved patterns to future days.
  5. Reality Check: the saved patterns are applied to real days they never saw, to see whether they would actually have been right.

Why the app is so strict

When you test hundreds of thousands of combinations, some will look impressive purely by luck, the way someone among thousands of coin-tossers will flip ten heads in a row. That is why patterns are learned on old years, checked on newer years, and compared with a luck check (the same search on deliberately scrambled data). The Reality Check tab is the final judge.

Glossary

Surya Siddhanta
A classical Indian astronomy text. Its model of planetary motion is used in traditional panchangs. It differs from modern astronomy by a few degrees for some planets (more for Venus near its retrograde), because the text's corrections (bija) are not applied.
Ayanamsa
The gap between the Western (season-based) and Vedic (star-based) zodiacs, about 24°. Lahiri is the Indian government standard; Raman, KP and others differ slightly.
Rashi / sign
One of 12 equal parts of the sky (Mesha/Aries … Meena/Pisces).
Nakshatra and pada
27 lunar mansions (Ashwini … Revati), each split into 4 padas.
Navamsa (D9)
The divisional chart formed by splitting each sign into nine parts.
Vakri (retrograde)
A planet appearing to move backwards for a while.
Asta (combust)
A planet too close to the Sun to be seen.
Uchcha / neecha / swakshetra
Exalted, debilitated and own-sign placements.
Graha drishti
Vedic aspects by sign: every planet aspects the 7th sign from itself; Mars also the 4th and 8th, Jupiter the 5th and 9th, Saturn the 3rd and 10th.
Degree aspects
Planets a set angle apart: together (0°), 60°, 90°, 120°, opposite (180°).
Panchang
The five limbs of the day: tithi (lunar day), vara (weekday), nakshatra, yoga and karana.
Lagna
The sign rising on the eastern horizon at the market's city (Mumbai by default) at that moment. Houses can be counted from it, or from the Moon's sign.
Learning / checking / final-test years
The history split in three. Patterns are found only in the learning years, must keep working in the checking years, and the final-test years are never used for searching.
Luck check
The search re-run on the same prices shifted in time, so the planets cannot match them. Real patterns must clearly beat what this finds.
Strictness
Strict keeps only patterns very unlikely to be luck. Balanced and Exploratory keep more patterns, including many lucky ones, so always run a Reality Check on them.
Patterns from the past do not guarantee anything about the future. Research tool, not financial advice.
